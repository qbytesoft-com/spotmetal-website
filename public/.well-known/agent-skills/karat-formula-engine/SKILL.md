# SpotMetal Karat Formula Engine Skill

Allows AI agents to calibrate complex jewelry manufacturing formulas and manage wholesale safety thresholds.

## Features
- Standard purity ratios: 24K (0.999), 22K (0.916), 18K (0.750), 14K (0.585), 10K (0.417), 9K (0.375), 8K (0.333), Sterling Silver (0.925).
- Tiered weight brackets: Light rings (0–5g), medium chains (5–20g), heavy bars (20g+).
- Craftsmanship making charges: Flat dollar markup, percentage markup, or per-gram fabrication fees.
